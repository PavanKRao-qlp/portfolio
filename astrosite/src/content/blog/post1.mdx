# Welcome to My Blog

## What You’ll Find Here

This blog will serve as a space where I:

- Document my journey in **game development** and **engine programming** (like my ongoing work on the _Umbra Engine_).
- Share **insights, tips, and tutorials** on programming in C++ and C#, performance optimization, and engine design.
- Reflect on **projects I’ve worked on**, challenges I’ve faced, and lessons learned along the way.

## Why This Blog Exists

I wanted a place not just to showcase my portfolio, but also to write more casually about the **process** behind my work. Code can feel very technical and rigid, but game development is as much about creativity, experimentation, and learning as it is about performance and structure.

This blog is my way of bridging both worlds—sharing what I know, documenting what I’m building, and hopefully connecting with others who share the same passion for games and technology.

Thanks for being here, and I hope you enjoy following along! ✨
