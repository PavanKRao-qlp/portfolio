**Hide In Hide** is a small rhythm-based game I built back in college with a few friends.  
The idea was simple but fun: you play as a wolf sneaking into a herd of sheep.  
To stay disguised, you have to hit the right inputs on the beat, mess it up, and your cover’s blown and the shepard will shoot you.

<Image src={myImage} alt="Hide in Hide" />
It started out as a game jam project where we just wanted to experiment with rhythm mechanics and 3d voxel based visuals.
In this project learnt a lot.
I worked on the following 
- creating endless scrolling sections for the level
- handling gesture inputs and mapping them to actions 
- scoring, general bugs and development
